---

# 1. Purpose

The Email Notification module provides a centralized, secure, tenant-aware email communication platform for all transactional, operational, security, workflow, reporting and customer communications generated by the Enterprise Workforce Platform.

It supports branded email templates, scheduling, delivery tracking, provider abstraction, localization, user preferences and enterprise-grade auditing.

---

# 2. Objectives

The subsystem shall:

- Support transactional and operational emails.
- Support multi-tenant branding.
- Support multilingual templates.
- Support scheduled delivery.
- Support retries and failover.
- Track delivery lifecycle.
- Support provider abstraction.
- Maintain complete audit history.
