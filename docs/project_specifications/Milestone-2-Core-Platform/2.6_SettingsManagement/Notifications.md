**Module:** Core Platform → Settings  
**Document:** Notifications  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved for Detailed Design  
**Owner:** Platform Architecture Team

---

# 1. Purpose

The Notification Settings module centralizes configuration, delivery, routing, preferences, templates, and governance for every notification generated by the Enterprise Workforce Platform.

It provides a configurable, tenant-aware communication framework supporting employees, managers, customer users, tenant administrators, and platform administrators.

Notifications are event-driven and integrate with Authentication, Attendance, GPS, Workflow, Fault Management, Lead Management, User Management, White Label, Reporting, and Audit.

---

# 2. Objectives

The subsystem shall:

- Support multi-channel notifications.
- Support tenant-specific branding.
- Support configurable templates.
- Support event-driven delivery.
- Support user notification preferences.
- Support retries and delivery tracking.
- Support multilingual templates.
- Maintain tenant isolation.
