# Business Notifications Module

## Enterprise Multi-Tenant Workforce Management SaaS Platform

**Version:** 2.0 Enterprise\
**Status:** Production Ready\
**Module:** Business Notifications

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Purpose

The Business Notifications module provides a centralized, configurable,
event-driven communication platform for the entire Workforce Management
SaaS application. It enables every business module to deliver real-time,
scheduled, and workflow-based notifications through multiple
communication channels while maintaining tenant isolation, branding,
localization, auditing, and enterprise-grade scalability.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Business Objectives

-   Centralize all business communications.
-   Eliminate hard-coded notifications.
-   Support multi-channel communication.
-   Improve employee engagement.
-   Improve workflow transparency.
-   Reduce missed approvals.
-   Increase SLA compliance.
-   Enable configurable tenant-specific communication.
-   Support white-label deployments.
-   Provide delivery analytics.
