# Reports & Analytics Module

## Overview

The Reports & Analytics module is the centralized reporting engine for
the Enterprise Multi-Tenant Workforce Management SaaS Platform. It
provides configurable dashboards, operational reports, KPI tracking,
scheduled reporting, exports, and analytics across all platform modules.

This module is platform-centric and works across Attendance, GPS, Fault
Management, Lead Management, User Management, Security, Audit, and
future modules.

## Objectives

-   Deliver real-time operational visibility
-   Provide tenant-specific analytics
-   Support configurable dashboards
-   Enable scheduled reports
-   Export reports to Excel, PDF, and CSV
-   Provide role-based access to reports
-   Support white-label branding
-   Scale to millions of records
