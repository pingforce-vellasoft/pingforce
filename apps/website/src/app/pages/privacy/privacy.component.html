Location Data and Background Tracking

PingForce collects and uses precise location data (GPS) to enable core workforce management features, such as tracking employee visits, attendance verification, and on-site task completion.

Background Location Collection

We collect location data even when the app is closed or not in use (running in the background). This is strictly necessary to maintain continuous tracking of your work route and ensure accurate reporting between your official "Check-in" and "Check-out" times.

  • When it is active: Background tracking is only active after you explicitly tap "Check-in" or start a tracked shift.
  • When it stops: Background tracking completely stops the moment you tap "Check-out" or end your shift. The app will never track your location outside of your designated working hours or active shifts.
  • Data Usage & Sharing: This data is used exclusively by your employer/management for operational purposes (e.g., verifying visit locations). It is securely transmitted and stored, and is never sold to third parties or used for advertising.

You will see a persistent notification in your device's status bar whenever background location tracking is actively running.
